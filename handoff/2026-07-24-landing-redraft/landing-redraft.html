Why the number is right

Right on the walls that break other tools.

Square walls are easy — anything prices those. Bids are won and lost on the ugly ones: gables and rakes, steps, arched openings, brick over block, corner returns. TMM lays the actual bond through all of it, so the pieces, the cuts, and the grout are counted — not factored.

Lays the bond, counts the pieces

Corners interlock course by course. Bond beams wrap. Lintels land on coursing. Rakes cut where the roof cuts them. No cut under 4" — the engine makes it work the way a foreman would.
