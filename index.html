Section 02 · What the engine knows

The rules nobody else bothered to encode.

  1. 01

    Corners interlock. Inside and outside. Bond runs through.

    No butt joints by default. The corner is a consequence of the bond, not a special case bolted on top.

  2. 02

    Non-modular leftover absorbs into the corner.

    The leg isn't fixed — it moves 8" every course, the way the bond actually runs. No 4" cut exposed in the field.

  3. 03

    Control joints default to 24'-8". Not 25'.

    296" = 18.5 blocks × 16" exactly. The modular spacing. Drag to override. Never auto-placed within 16" of a corner.

  4. 04

    Rebar drives bond beams. Bond beams aren't a manual choice.

    Set the schedule from the structural drawings. Courses snap. Dev-length extension past openings. Weight reported in pounds, not just LF.

  5. 05

    Half-high courses auto-detected.

    Wall isn't modular 8"? Engine generates the half-high at top or bottom and adds it as its own line item. MasonryIQ doesn't.

  6. 06

    No cut under 4" gets produced. Ever.

    If the layout would force it, the engine absorbs into adjacent joints or shifts the cut to the next valid position. By design.

  7. 07

    10" and 12" CMU corners use an 8" block + face shell spacer.

    Separate takeoff line items, the way the supplier ships them and the way the crew lays them.

  8. 08

    Mason override is one click.

    The engine is right 90% of the time. You're right on the other 10%. Click the block, change the type, the takeoff updates.
